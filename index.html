Courageously changing course.

ORA is a consulting firm dedicated to organisational courage & optimism for the future. We work with owner-managed companies, SMEs, and leadership teams that need to adapt to a changing environment. Instead of ready-made answers and trend reports, we bring extensive methodological expertise, entrepreneurial acumen, and sharp foresight. After all, the future isn’t a fixed plan, but a constant act of balance. Forward-thinking organisations remain attentive, regularly reviewing their priorities and adjusting their course as necessary.

We support you in this process: we bring together the knowledge and experience already present in your organisation and create clear direction and define future directed steps.

Forward-thinking that provides direction

We scan your environment for subtle signals, noticeable trends, and fundamental drivers. This creates an overview of what’s coming and where it’s coming from. Methodologically, we rely on strategic foresight, futures literacy, and innovation research. But we don’t stop at simply understanding change.

orient to new directions

Entrepreneurship That Drives Change

We think like entrepreneurs. We understand strategy, read financial statements, and know what it means to lead a company. For SMEs, traditional strategic approaches and consulting models are often too detached from day-to-day operations. What’s needed is a pragmatic, hands-on mindset that grounds the future in reality and translates it into concrete actions and decisions.

move courageously adjusting the course

An Understanding of People That Brings Them Along

We still believe in humans:
A company is only as good as its employees.
If employees aren’t brought along, a change in course can easily lead to failure. We have a keen sense of the sociocultural dynamics within organisations and know how to inspire, engage, and empower diverse interests and personalities.

ORA combines expertise from these three fields into a pragmatic process and a set of navigation tools: a map of the changing environment, an organisational profile/ship (tangible and intangible resources), and a compass that provides direction and sets the course.
